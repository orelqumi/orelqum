Privacy and enquiry information.

How the current Orelqumi demonstration website handles personal details.

What this website does

The enquiry forms prepare a message in your email application. They do not send data to a server or store submitted details on the site. You choose whether to send the email.

Information you may share

Depending on the enquiry, you may include your name, contact details, country, project requirements, course interest and any message you write.

External providers

Photographs are loaded from Unsplash image servers, and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive ordinary browser request information under their respective privacy policies.

Before launching publicly

The owner should provide a verified contact address and replace this demonstration notice with a complete operational privacy policy covering enquiry handling, retention, data subject rights and applicable laws.
