PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy and enquiry information.

How Orelqumi handles website enquiries and personal details.

What this website does

When you submit an enquiry, the details you provide are transmitted to Orelqumi’s Cloudflare-hosted enquiry service and stored in its restricted-access CRM database. Your information is used to respond to your enquiry and manage potential website or training engagements.

Information you may share

Depending on the enquiry, you may include your name, contact details, country, project requirements, course interest and any message you write.

External providers

Photographs are loaded from Unsplash image servers, and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive ordinary browser request information under their respective privacy policies.

Before launching publicly

Enquiries are available to authorised Orelqumi administrators in the client management system. You may contact Orelqumi to request access, correction or deletion of submitted enquiry information. This notice should be reviewed and completed with contact details, an explicit retention schedule and any other disclosures required by applicable privacy laws before launching the service publicly.
