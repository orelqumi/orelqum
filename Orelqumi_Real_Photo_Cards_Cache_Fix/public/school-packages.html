COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Is KSh 13,000 a monthly payment?

No. It is the one-time starting development fee for the Starter School package.

Is hosting included?

No. Domain registration, hosting and third-party service fees are priced separately unless your written quotation states otherwise.

Can we add an online admission portal?

Yes, this can be assessed as an additional project. Custom application workflows and student management systems are not part of the basic package.

Do we have to subscribe to maintenance?

No. Maintenance is optional. You can request it after your website is launched.

Can we discuss the project online?

Yes. Request a Google Meet, phone, WhatsApp or local in-person consultation through the enquiry form. Meeting times are confirmed manually.
