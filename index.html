Ask us a question ↗
How much does a website cost?

Prices depend on the type of website and features. Portfolio websites start at KSh 8,000, business websites at KSh 13,000 and online stores at KSh 30,000. A final quote follows project scoping.

Can I request a custom website?

Yes. Tell us what you need through the quotation form, and we can review the requirements for a tailored proposal.

Can I estimate my budget first?

Yes. Our Build Your Website calculator lets you choose pages and features to see an indicative starting estimate.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Optional monthly website care is available. Maintenance pricing and inclusions are confirmed separately from development.
